## Decisions (guan)

- Scope of "usable": a few notebooks with a clear structure and no free globals; symbols that
  show what is done; the `acquire` / `analyze` / `display` protocol; a readable hot path. Out of
  scope: non-qsim shared infra and dormant/deprecated code, unless it is coupled into the MBR base.
- Order: 8A (MBR base), 8B (Matrix Pencil), 8D (measurement notebooks), 8C (analysis notebook).
- The `'decoder'` mode and `storage_phase_matrix` go to `deprecated/`. They came from per-pulse AC
  Stark frame tracking. With Kerr, that frame and the final-half-pi frame differ by a gauge, and
  the final half-pi method was chosen because it was easier than fixing the per-pulse one.
- Palindrome stays: it is a symmetric (second-order) Trotter step.
- SFF on the spectrum stays: `analyze_sff` is |Tr U(t) / D|^2 from the diagonal returns, the
  direct measurement. Decision 7-3 was about the disorder-ensemble SFF.
