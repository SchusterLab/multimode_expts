## What was found

1. **Dressed K = -17 to -21 kHz at both N = 2 and N = 3**, walks and MBR agree on one config.
   MBR (g free per N): K(2) = -19.1 +/- 0.6, K(3) = -16.6 +/- 0.9 kHz. Walks: -20.8 / -20.1
   (with M1 dephasing), -18.8 / -17.5 (loss only). The spread is the walk damping model.
2. **The effective coupling grows with photon number**: g/g_saved = 0.991 / 1.011 / 1.040 (MBR)
   and 0.991 / 1.012 / 1.033-1.038 (walks) at N = 1 / 2 / 3; the 10-17 walks had the same
   N=1 -> 2 step. With g fixed, this appears as K(N=3) of about -30 kHz: that is the "different
   Kerr at N=3". The weak 4th N=3 MBR line (weight 0.03) separates the two descriptions: g fixed
   (+ K, + K3) predicts the 3rd spacing at 97.7 kHz, measured 94.0 +/- 1.1 (chi2 about 13 for 2 dof);
   g free fits (chi2 0.02, 1 dof). A repeat with 1000 reps (JOB-20261024-00073, 08:20) points
   the same way but weaker: g fixed chi2 4.3 (2 dof), g free K = -19.2 +/- 1.5 kHz, g/g_saved 1.029.
   Together g fixed: chi2 16.9 for 4 dof (p about 0.002). Favoured, not yet firm; the two g-free
   values differ by 2.8 sigma (drift?).
3. **No evidence for a higher-order M1 nonlinearity**: K3 = 0.6 +/- 3.8 (MBR N=3) and
   0.8 +/- 0.6 kHz (walks N=2+3).
4. **The detuning sign in the code is right.** K and g fixed to the MBR values, chi2/dof for
   sign -1 (storage onsite energy = -detuning, as in `fixed_n_hamiltonian`) vs +1: 4.3 vs 14.4,
   10.0 vs 16.8, 6.4 vs 36.2. jonginn's 10-17 W-scan gives K < 0 under sign -1 at every
   well-measured W (30-90 kHz).
5. Walk K is model-dependent: for the 10-17 walks, adding M1 dephasing moved K(N=2) from -17 to
   -23 kHz, and with dephasing the 10-17 N=3 walk does not constrain K at all.
6. The 10-16 cavity Ramsey gain sweeps (undriven) do not give a usable K: saved fits from -1.4
   to +24 kHz at 0.25 mA, discrete fitted frequencies. Not re-measured.
